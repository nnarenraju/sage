Collaboration
=============

Sage is an active research project and we are always keen to work with new
collaborators. Whether you want to deploy Sage on your own dataset, adapt it
to a new detector or custom GW source, or contribute to the codebase, we would
love to hear from you.

----

What we can do together
------------------------

.. grid:: 1 1 2 2
   :gutter: 3

   .. grid-item-card:: :octicon:`telescope` Custom GW searches
      :text-align: center

      Deploy Sage for your specific detector configuration or custom GW source.
      The pipeline is not limited to CBC signals — we are actively interested
      in extending to other source classes including continuous waves, bursts,
      and beyond.

   .. grid-item-card:: :octicon:`beaker` Joint research
      :text-align: center

      Collaborate on extending the methodology — new architectures, bias
      studies, parameter estimation, or multi-messenger follow-up.

   .. grid-item-card:: :octicon:`tools` Technical support
      :text-align: center

      Get help setting up the data pipeline, configuring the training loop,
      or interpreting diagnostic outputs for your use case.

   .. grid-item-card:: :octicon:`git-pull-request` Code contributions
      :text-align: center

      Contribute new waveform models, noise strategies, or DSP components.
      See the `GitHub repository <https://github.com/nnarenraju/sage>`__ for
      open issues and contribution guidelines.
